# HEMS-2000 Pro Home Energy Inverter Manual

## 1. Safety Instructions
- **High Voltage**: Do not touch terminals while operating.
- **Emergency Stop**: Press the Red Button on the side panel to cut off all power immediately.

## 2. Operating Modes
- **ECO Mode**: Optimizes for lowest cost. Charges battery during off-peak hours (00:00-06:00).
- **FAST Mode**: Prioritizes charging speed. Draws max grid power (up to 11kW).
- **UPS Mode**: Keeps battery at 100% for outage protection.

## 3. Troubleshooting & Error Codes
- **Error E-101 (Grid Loss)**: The grid voltage is outside the allowed range. Check AC breaker.
- **Error E-501 (Over Temperature)**: Internal temperature > 85°C. Fan may be blocked. Clean the air intake.
- **Error E-502 (Battery Comm Fail)**: CAN communication with BMS lost. Check the communication cable between Inverter and Battery.
- **Warning W-001 (Fan Speed)**: Cooling fan is running at max speed. This is normal during high loads (>8kW).

## 4. Maintenance
- Inspect air filters every 6 months.
- Firmware updates are automatic via OTA if connected to Wi-Fi.
